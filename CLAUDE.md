# Blog writing rules

Applies to every post written or edited in `_posts/`.

- **Ideas, not reporting.** Develop our own argument. Don't recap who said what.
- **No quotes, no names.** Don't quote people or attribute views to them ("X argues…", "as Y put it"). Don't announce that something is a reference or quote.
- **Cite with a short ref note.** A source gets a link and a terse note, e.g. `Ref: [thread](url)` or a `### Refs` list at the end. Nothing more.
- **Minimum words, dense ideas.** Cut setup, transitions, hedging and restatement. Prefer tables, lists and diagrams over prose when they carry the idea in fewer words.
- **Front matter:** `layout: post`, `author`/`authors` key from `_config.yml`, `categories`, a one-line `teaser`, `image`, `toc: true` for long posts.
